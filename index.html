01About FCC

About FCC (Philippines) Corp.

Since 1993 we have built clutch components at Laguna Technopark — to the engineering and quality standards of FCC Co., Ltd., Japan. Every part we make carries the power from the engine to the road.

Laguna Technopark, Biñan Tsuyoshi Nakada · President

Built in Laguna. Engineered to Japanese standard.

Incorporated on 27 September 1993, FCC (Philippines) Corp. is a wholly owned subsidiary of FCC Co., Ltd. of Japan and a PEZA registered enterprise. The plant produces clutch assemblies and components for two-wheel applications, cylinder comp, and friction material for four-wheel clutch systems.
